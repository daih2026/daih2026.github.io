Sponsorship Principles

Transparent Support

DAIH follows conference best practices to keep support transparent and to preserve the independence of the workshop program.

  1. The program committees determine the workshop substance and structure independently.
  2. Submitted work is selected through rigorous double-blind peer review with conflict-of-interest disclosure.
  3. All financial support will be publicly acknowledged.
  4. Sponsor benefits are disclosed below.
  5. Corporate sponsors may not direct how their contributions are spent.
